About

I write stories
you disappear into.

I'm Anika — a writer, archer, and aspiring builder of adventure writing retreats. My stories tend to live in the hazy space between the real and the mythic: forests that feel too alive, people who feel too lost, mornings that feel like they're holding their breath.

When I'm not writing, I'm planning the retreat where writers can do exactly what I do — live inside the story for a little while.
